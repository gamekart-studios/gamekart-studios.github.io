Sugar Pairs – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Gamekart Studios ("we", "us") makes the mobile game Sugar Pairs (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App uses and why. The App is designed for children and families, and we take children's privacy seriously.

1. Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • There are no user accounts, chat or social features.
  • The App does not use the Android Advertising ID.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, settings and daily gifts) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads using Google AdMob. All ad requests are marked as directed to children (COPPA) and under the age of consent (GDPR), and are limited to family-friendly (G-rated) content. Ads are non-personalized: they are not based on a child's behaviour or interests and are not used for tracking across apps. To deliver ads and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical data such as IP address, device type and operating system version.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played so we can improve it, for example which levels are started or completed and which power-ups are used. This data is anonymous: it uses a random app-instance identifier, not the Advertising ID, and is never used for advertising or personalization (ad storage, ad user data and ad personalization are turned off).

Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. Children's privacy

Sugar Pairs is intended for children and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App follows the Google Play Families Policy, and the ad and analytics services above are configured for child-directed use. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Analytics data is kept by Google for a limited period and then deleted automatically. You can stop all data collection at any time by uninstalling the App. You may also contact us to request deletion of any data related to your device.

7. Security

We rely on Google's services, which protect data in transit with encryption.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
